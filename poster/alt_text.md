## Column 1 — WHY & HOW: UK Biobank imaging cohort, three data domains, one model
- **Background & questions.** Two bullets (CAD is the leading cause of death, ~50% first present as a heart attack; clinical scores omit genetics and imaging and how much imaging and genetic risk overlap was unknown) and three numbered questions (which imaging modalities add to clinical, metabolic, ECG and genetic factors; overlap between imaging-derived and polygenic risk; sequential integration for discrimination, calibration, stratification).
- **Figure 1 of the manuscript** (raster, 10.85 × 14.66 in, 150 ppi): the multimodal framework. A timeline (baseline data 2006–2010, imaging 2014–2024, prediction window 2010–2024) feeds three branches — Medical Imaging (3D cardiac MRI: long axis, blood flow, aortic distensibility, ResNet-18 with MedicalNet pretraining; 2D liver MRI, pancreatic MRI and whole-body DXA, ResNet-50 with RadImageNet pretraining), Genetics (genotype data, GWAS summary statistics from FinnGen, MVP and UK Biobank, METAL meta-analysis of 229k cases and 1.03M controls, PRS-CS) and ECG and Clinical (machine-derived ECG features, blood biochemistry, demographic and lifestyle data). Embeddings and PRS pass through PCA into a tabular feature table with spatially cross-validated train/test splits, then into tree-based models for CAD classification and a Cox proportional-hazards model for risk stratification (survival curves for low, intermediate and high risk).
- **Six cohort tiles** stacked beside the figure: 42,351 UK Biobank imaging participants; 677 incident CAD (1.6%); 4.6 yr median follow-up; 211,755 cardiac cine MRI series; 127,053 2D images (liver, pancreas, DXA); 10,597 held-out test set (178 cases).
- Italic note under the figure: cardiac MRI does not image coronary atherosclerosis; its signal reflects remodeling, vascular stiffness and cardiometabolic burden. Outcome line: clinically recognized CAD (MI, angina, revascularization); prevalent cases excluded.
- **Methods in brief.** Four bullets: encoders fine-tuned on balanced case–control training data only; embeddings → PCA, 10 PCs per stream (80 total); PRS-CS from a European meta-GWAS, OR 1.87 per SD, AUC 0.766 (with age, sex, 20 PCs); XGBoost + nested Cox, imaging risk score = cross-fitted ridge Cox.

## Column 2 — IMAGING REPRESENTATIONS: Deep embeddings beat handcrafted cardiac phenotypes
- **Hero number** "0.770 vs 0.666" (orange, 92 pt): AUC of deep embeddings vs conventional imaging-derived phenotypes (IDPs); both combined 0.775.
- **Horizontal bar chart** (full card width, 5 in tall), AUC axis 0.60–0.80: Conventional IDPs 0.666 (grey), Deep embeddings 0.770 (orange), Both combined 0.775 (dark slate). Annotations: embeddings vs IDPs LR = 414.5, p = 4.1×10⁻⁷⁶; adding IDPs to embeddings LR = 45.1, p = 4.6×10⁻⁶. Caption: embeddings outperform IDPs; adding IDPs barely helps (logistic models; plateau at ~5 PCs per modality; Fig. 2a,b).
- **Horizontal bar chart** of canonical correlation (CV1) between embeddings and IDPs, axis 0.60–0.90: 2-chamber long axis 0.812, 3-chamber 0.789, 4-chamber 0.841, aortic distensibility 0.816 (all orange). Caption: embeddings encode physiology — CV1 loads on stroke volume, ejection fraction, myocardial mass and wall thickness (Fig. 2c).
- **Model-selection strip**, two panels. Left, AUC of 3D cardiac encoders by pretraining: 3D ResNet-18 0.608, MC3-18 0.618, R(2+1)D 0.650 (grey, Kinetics video) and Med3D 0.681 (orange, selected). Right, dumbbells of 2D-encoder accuracy from ImageNet (grey dot) to RadImageNet (orange dot): liver MRI 0.50 → 0.64, pancreas MRI 0.53 → 0.63, DXA 0.54 → 0.57. Caption: medical pretraining wins (Supp. Fig. 3b, 4b).

## Column 4 — INTEGRATION & STRATIFICATION: Sequential integration finds very-high-risk people
- **Hero number** "36.8%" with its subtitle on the line beneath: 5-yr CAD incidence in the top quintile of both imaging and genetic risk within Tier 3; cohort 1.6%. To the right, four tiles: 0.634 continuous NRI vs clinical (95% CI 0.534–0.734); 9.5% index of prediction accuracy for the full model (clinical 5.5%); 86.8 yr median heart age in Tier 3 (cohort 55.3 yr); 0.99–1.08 calibration slopes of the nested models.
- **Horizontal bar chart** of held-out C-index (axis from 0.55; bold labels = nested models): PRS alone† 0.621 (blue), SCORE2 0.722, Pooled Cohort Equations 0.725, Clinical 0.749 (grey), Imaging alone 0.772 (orange), Clinical + PRS 0.788, PRS + Imaging 0.789, Clinical + Imaging 0.813, Full: Clinical + PRS + Imaging 0.819 (dark slate). Caption: each layer adds discrimination; imaging is among the strongest independent predictors (HR ≈ 2.1 per SD); HR per SD in the full model: imaging ≈ 2.1, PRS ≈ 1.7, demographics ≈ 1.4, lipids ≈ 1.1; †PRS alone = score without age/sex, whereas the AUC 0.766 in column 1 includes age, sex and 20 PCs.
- **Staircase bar chart** of 5-yr CAD incidence under sequential stratification: overall cohort 1.6% (grey), Tier 1 clinical 8.0% (grey), Tier 2 + PRS 11.3% (blue), Tier 3 + imaging 15.9% (orange), top quintile of both within Tier 3 36.8% (dark slate). A bracket beside the tallest bar reads "≈23× the cohort event rate". Caption: sequential tiers enrich risk far beyond clinical stratification (held-out set); leave-one-centre-out AUC 0.785–0.822 and C-index 0.73–0.76 — discrimination is stable across the four UK Biobank imaging centres (site-level robustness, not external validation).
- **Conclusions** (four bullets): imaging and polygenic risk are nearly uncorrelated, complementary axes; long-axis cine and aortic distensibility MRI carry the incremental signal while blood-flow, liver, pancreas and DXA add little once clinical variables are included; clinical → PRS → imaging integration isolates a 36.8% 5-yr-risk subgroup; a framework for complementary risk, not yet a clinical screening tool.
- **Limitations** (three bullets): no CAC/CTA in UK Biobank, so no calcium-score benchmark; European-ancestry, complete-case, healthier-than-general cohort with covariates measured at imaging, so part of imaging's gain may reflect temporal proximity; not externally validated, observational — prospective trials needed.
